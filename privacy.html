Kolomto Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Kolomto (“the app”, “we”) is a private, non-commercial home music service run by one person for a small, invitation-only group of family and friends. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, where it is kept and how users can delete it. It applies to the Kolomto web app and to its “Move your music” import feature, including the data it receives from Google APIs.

1. Data received from Google (YouTube Data API)

When a user chooses to import their music from YouTube / YouTube Music and signs in with Google, the app requests one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly (“View your YouTube account”). With it the app reads only:

  • the user's playlists: playlist titles and the list of videos in each playlist;
  • the user's liked videos (this list includes YouTube Music likes);
  • for each listed video: its title, channel / artist name, duration and video ID;
  • the YouTube channel name of the account, shown to the user so they can confirm which account was imported.

The app does not access the user's e-mail, contacts, viewing or search history, comments, subscriptions, uploads, messages or any other Google data, and it never makes changes on YouTube.

2. How the Google data is used

The data is used for one purpose only: to recreate the user's liked songs and playlists inside the same user's Kolomto account. The app matches each song (by title, artist and duration) against its music library and music catalogs, then marks the matched songs as liked and builds the playlists in their original order. Songs that cannot be matched are shown to the user, who decides what to do with them.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, selling, or training machine-learning / AI models, and it is not shown to other users.

3. Sharing

Data received from Google is not sold, rented or transferred to third parties. To find a song, the app sends search queries consisting only of the song's title and artist name to public music catalogs (for example Deezer or YouTube search); these queries contain nothing that identifies the user or their Google account.

4. Storage and security

  • All data is stored on a single private server owned and operated by the app's administrator. It is reachable only through encrypted connections (HTTPS or a private VPN).
  • The Google access token is stored encrypted and is used only while the import runs. By default it is deleted automatically when the import finishes. A user may choose to keep it to re-sync the import later and can delete it at any moment with the “Forget sign-in” button.
  • The imported lists (song titles, artists, video IDs and how they were matched) are kept in the user's import record so the user can review the result, until the user deletes the import.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Delete an import in the app (“Move your music” → the import → Delete) to remove its imported lists and any stored token.
  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the app can no longer read anything from your account.
  • Ask the administrator to delete your whole Kolomto account and all data linked to it (see Contact). Requests are handled within 30 days.

6. Other data the app keeps

To work as a music service, the app stores the user's username, a hashed password, liked songs, playlists, listening history used for personal recommendations, and settings. A single session cookie keeps the user signed in; there are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. This website (the page you are reading) uses no cookies or analytics.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Kolomto's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Kolomto uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube import you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

Kolomto is not directed to children under 13, and accounts are created only by invitation of the administrator.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/danciksaparbekov-arch/danciksaparbekov-arch.github.io/issues or write to the support e-mail shown on the Google sign-in screen of Kolomto.
